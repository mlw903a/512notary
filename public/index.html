What does the $75 visit include?

The proposed pilot price covers travel and up to five routine notarizations for one signer. More than five notarizations, additional signers, witnesses, or special appointments require a separate quote before booking. This test does not collect payment.

What should I have ready?

Have your documents and identification ready, with any required witnesses arranged. Check the document issuer’s instructions. The notary determines whether the requested notarization can proceed.

What if my area or time is unavailable?

Our planned hours are Monday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Central. Nearby addresses and alternative times can be submitted as saved test requests. These do not reserve a visit or send a message.

Can I get help choosing or preparing a document?

Notaries do not provide legal advice or select legal documents. Ask the document issuer or an attorney for instructions about your document and the notarial act required.
